Connection Liners

Flexible polyester knitted hose with thermoplastic coating for the rehabilitation of lateral connections.

Restores lateral and branch connections at 45°, 90° and 180° from DN 50 to DN 300 — supplied stitched and sealed, or stitched only.

  • DN 50 – 300
  • 45° · 90° · 180°
  • Approx. 3.0 mm

About this product

The Connection Liner is built for non-pressure pipelines, restoring the junction between a lateral and the main with a durable polyester knitted hose under a thermoplastic coating. Angles other than 45°, 90° and 180°, and custom sizes, can be supplied in consultation with Lateral Repairs.

The finished quality depends on the resin system used, the inversion pressure and the curing pressure — we advise against other resin systems or higher pressures. All figures are guideline values determined under laboratory conditions and can differ on site.
