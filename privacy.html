2. What data we process, and why

2.1 Contact enquiries

When you send us an enquiry by email or phone, we process the details you provide — your name, email address and the content of your message — in order to respond to you and prepare a quotation. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract) and, for other enquiries, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to correspondence). We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up business relationship, after which it is deleted. The contact form on this website is not connected at present, so enquiries currently reach us only by email or phone; this section will be updated before the form is connected.

2.3 Embedded videos (YouTube)

Our company video is embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only after you actively choose to play it and consent to third-party content. Until then, no connection to Google is made. If you play the video, Google (YouTube) processes your IP address and may set cookies under its own privacy policy. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

We use no analytics, no advertising trackers and no marketing cookies on this website. Fonts, scripts and images are served from our own domain.

All traffic to this website is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS, enforced with HSTS). We apply a strict Content Security Policy, modern browser protections against clickjacking and content-sniffing, and we minimise third-party services. We share personal data only with: our hosting provider (Serveriai.lt, as processor); our email provider when you correspond with us; and Google (YouTube) only if you consent to video playback. We do not sell personal data.

6. Your rights

To exercise these rights, contact info@lateralrepairs.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, vdai.lrv.lt).
