2. What data we process, and why

2.1 Contact enquiries

When you send an enquiry through our contact form or by email, we process the details you provide — your name, email address and the content of your message — in order to respond to you and prepare a quotation. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract) and, for other enquiries, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to correspondence). We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up business relationship, after which it is deleted.

2.2 Hosting & server logs

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (San Francisco, USA). When you visit the site, the hosting provider technically processes your IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps in server logs, as is necessary to deliver the website securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in operating a secure, functioning website). Transfers of data to the USA by Vercel are safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and Vercel's participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Log data is retained only for a short period and is not used to identify individual visitors.

2.3 Embedded videos (YouTube)

Our company video is embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only after you actively choose to play it and consent to third-party content. Until then, no connection to Google is made. If you play the video, Google (YouTube) processes your IP address and may set cookies under its own privacy policy. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

2.4 What we do not do

We use no analytics, no advertising trackers and no marketing cookies on this website. Fonts, scripts and images are served from our own domain.

3. Cookies and local storage

Details of the (minimal) storage this site uses are set out in our Cookie Policy.

4. Data security

All traffic to this website is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS, enforced with HSTS). We apply a strict Content Security Policy, modern browser protections against clickjacking and content-sniffing, and we minimise third-party services. Access to enquiry correspondence is limited to staff who need it to respond.

5. Recipients

We share personal data only with: our hosting provider (Vercel, as processor); our email provider when you correspond with us; and Google (YouTube) only if you consent to video playback. We do not sell personal data.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and — where processing is based on consent — to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To exercise these rights, contact info@lateralrepairs.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, vdai.lrv.lt).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or legal requirements change. The current version, with its date, is always available on this page.
