Resin systems

A liner is only as good as the resin inside it. We supply the matching resin for every job — the difference between them is working time: how long the crew has to impregnate, invert and position the liner before it starts to cure.

LR-Epoxy Fastcast 15

Our fastest two-part epoxy. Built for small drain and lateral repairs where the priority is getting the line back into service the same visit.

LR-Epoxy Fastcast 30

The same fast-cast chemistry with a longer working window — extra time to impregnate and place the liner, while still curing quickly on site.

LR-120+

Our longest working time, for large-diameter and manhole-to-manhole runs where a big liner has to be soaked and installed without racing the clock.

LR-UV-Resin

Light-cured resin for UV liner systems. It stays workable until the UV light train is switched on, so the crew decides exactly when curing starts.

30 April 2026 — Group announcement

Lateral Repairs joins the newly founded group around IMS Trenchless & Polypipe.

European specialist technology companies have joined forces to create a leading integrated platform in the trenchless pipe rehabilitation sector — a true partnership between founders and financial partner Apheon. Lateral Repairs Managing Director Drew Holland joins the shareholding of the group, bringing our CIPP liner manufacturing into a platform that covers the entire no-dig value chain: robots, liners, resins, tools and curing systems.

IMS Robotics

Sewer rehabilitation robots, milling systems and cutter technology — the robotic backbone of the group.
